Research Interests

Spatial Intelligence

Spatial reasoning and mental modeling from limited observations, enabling AI systems to understand object relationships, scene structure, and navigable 3D environments.

Multimodal Reasoning

Developing vision-language models that reason across images, 3D representations, and language, with applications in spatial understanding, scene interpretation, and human-centric reasoning.

3D Generative AI

Scene-to-3D generation, geometry-aware reconstruction, and adaptation of 3D foundation models for real-world and industrial applications.
